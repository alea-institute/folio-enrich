Legal Memorandum: Breach of Exclusive Distribution Agreement

Client: Distribuidora Atlântica Ltda., limited liability company, São Paulo, Brazil
Opposing Party: Viña Cordillera S.A., corporation, Santiago, Chile
Practice Area: International Arbitration; Commercial Litigation; Intellectual Property
Industry: Wine Industry; Food and Beverage; Import and Export
Governing Law: United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods; Chilean law
Dispute Resolution: ICC arbitration, three arbitrators, seat Lima, Peru
Claims: breach of contract; breach of warranty; wrongful termination; trademark infringement; unfair competition
Remedies Sought: direct damages; consequential damages; lost profits; prejudgment interest; preliminary injunction; accounting of profits

Background
Exclusive Distribution Agreement, five-year contract term. Supplier warranty: product specifications, certificate of origin, Brazilian import regulations, food safety regulations, labeling requirements. Payment: invoice, letter of credit, commercial bank, payment term, bill of lading.
Customs detention: Brazilian customs authority detained wine shipment; laboratory reports showed regulatory violation. Ministry of Agriculture administrative order: product recall; customs duties; regulatory fine; storage costs.
Supplier terminated Agreement without notice; appointed competing distributor, Rio de Janeiro; continued trademark license to competitor.

Legal Issues
1. Fundamental breach of contract: nonconforming goods, Article 25 CISG.
2. Wrongful termination without cause: notice period; exclusivity clause.
3. Damages: direct damages, consequential damages, lost profits, prejudgment interest; limitation of liability clause.
4. Interim relief: arbitral tribunal; emergency arbitrator; trademark infringement; unfair competition.

Analysis
Breach of contract and breach of warranty: laboratory reports, customs detention records, recall order establish nonconforming goods. Buyer remedies: declare contract avoided; claim damages; restitution of purchase price. Timely notice of nonconformity defeats waiver defense.
Wrongful termination: termination clause requires material breach, written notice, thirty-day cure period. Competing distributor appointment breaches exclusivity clause and implied covenant of good faith and fair dealing.
Damages: expectation damages, lost profits, contract term; expert testimony, forensic accountant; incidental damages, regulatory fine. Limitation of liability clause excludes gross negligence and willful misconduct. Force majeure clause: no defense.
Intellectual property: trademark sublicense, Brazil; likelihood of confusion; trademark infringement; unfair competition; accounting of profits.
Jurisdiction and procedure: arbitration clause, New York Convention; arbitral award enforcement, Brazil, Chile, Peru. ICC Rules: emergency arbitrator, interim measures. Brazilian court: preliminary injunction in aid of arbitration.
Defenses and risks: counterclaim, unpaid invoices, USD 480,000; statute of limitations defense; jurisdictional objection.
